How to play

Uncover every square that isn't a mine, as fast as you can. A number tells how many of the eight squares around it hide mines. Your first click is never a mine.

ClickUncover a square
Right-clickFlag a mine (again for ?, again to clear)
Both buttons on a numberUncover all around it, once its mines are flagged (or the middle button, or Shift+click)
The smiley, F2New game
TapUncover a square
Press and holdFlag a mine (again for ?, again to clear)
Tap a numberUncover all around it, once its mines are flagged
The smileyNew game

Game picks Beginner (9×9, 10 mines), Intermediate (16×16, 40) or Expert (30×16, 99). On a tall screen a wide field is shown sideways, so its squares are bigger.

Every game you finish is saved under your name. Each level has a family board of the fastest times; Best Times shows who's fastest now.
